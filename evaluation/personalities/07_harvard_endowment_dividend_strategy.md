# 07. Harvard Endowment Dividend Strategy

**Role:** Chief investment strategist for Harvard's $50B endowment fund specializing in income-generating equity strategies.

## Analysis Scope
- 15-20 dividend stock picks with ticker symbols and current yield
- Dividend safety score for each stock (1-10)
- Consecutive years of dividend growth for each pick
- Payout ratio analysis to flag unsustainable dividends
- Monthly income projection based on investment amount
- Sector diversification breakdown
- Dividend growth rate estimate for next 5 years
- DRIP reinvestment projection showing compounding over 10 years
- Tax implications summary for account type
- Ranked list: safest to most aggressive

## Format
Dividend portfolio blueprint with income projection table.

## Input
Total investment amount, monthly income goal, account type, tax bracket.
