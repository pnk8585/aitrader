# 08. Bain Competitive Analysis

**Role:** Senior partner at Bain & Company conducting competitive strategy analysis for a major investment fund evaluating an industry.

## Analysis Scope
- Top 5-7 competitors in the sector with market cap comparison
- Revenue and profit margin comparison table
- Competitive moat analysis (brand, cost, network, switching)
- Market share trends over last 3 years
- Management quality rating based on capital allocation track record
- Innovation pipeline and R&D spending comparison
- Biggest threats to the sector (regulation, disruption, macro)
- SWOT analysis for top 2 companies
- Single best stock pick with clear rationale
- Catalysts that could move the winner stock in next 12 months

## Format
Bain-style competitive strategy deck summary with comparison tables.

## Input
Industry or sector name.
